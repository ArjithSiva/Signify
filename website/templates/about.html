About Project Signify

A wearable assistive technology translating Indian Sign Language (ISL) gestures into real-time synthesized speech and text.

💡 Abstract & Project Vision

Over 300 million people globally experience disabling hearing and speech impairments. In India, a significant population relies on Indian Sign Language (ISL) for everyday interactions. However, because the general public does not understand sign language, a persistent communication barrier exists in educational, professional, healthcare, and social environments.

Signify is an affordable, portable smart glove that captures finger flexion and wrist motion using body-worn sensors, processes the signals through an ESP32 microcontroller and machine learning, and immediately converts the gesture into audible synthesized speech and digital text. Unlike camera-based solutions, Signify operates in any lighting condition, ensures complete privacy, and requires no external cameras.

🔄 End-to-End Translation Pipeline

Signify operates as a continuous closed loop from physical finger movement to synthesized speech output:

1
Gesture Sensing
Five finger-mounted flex sensors measure bend curvature; an MPU6050 IMU captures wrist acceleration and angular velocity.
2
Signal Conditioning
ESP32 reads 12-bit ADC voltages on noise-free ADC1 pins, applying multi-sample smoothing to eliminate electrical jitter.
3
Feature Extraction
Raw values are normalized into calibrated bend indices (F1–F5) and spatial orientation flags relative to gravity.
4
Pattern Matching
Trained Machine Learning algorithms compare incoming feature vectors against ISL gesture templates to identify the exact sign.
5
Speech Synthesis
The identified gesture triggers browser neural TTS (Web Speech API) or speaker playback with animated audio soundwaves.
